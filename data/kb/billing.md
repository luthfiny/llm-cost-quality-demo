# Billing

## Billing date
All subscriptions are billed on the 25th of each month.

## Payment methods
We accept QRIS, GoPay, OVO, BCA virtual account and credit cards. Credit cards and GoPay can be saved for automatic billing.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry 3 times over 5 days. If all retries fail, the subscription is paused automatically until you update your payment method.
