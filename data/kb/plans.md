# Plans

## Plan prices
The Basic plan costs IDR 89,000 per month and includes 2 bags of 200 g coffee. The Plus plan costs IDR 149,000 per month and includes 4 bags of 200 g coffee. The Office plan costs IDR 450,000 per month and includes 15 bags of 200 g coffee.

## Annual billing
Annual billing is available for every plan. Paying annually gives you 12 months for the price of 10 months. Annual plans are charged once, on the first billing date.

## Changing plans
You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the Account page. A plan change applies from the next billing date. Upgrades are not prorated for the current month.
