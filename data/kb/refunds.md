# Refunds and damaged items

## Damaged bags
If a bag arrives damaged, send a photo to support within 48 hours of delivery. We send a free replacement bag with your next shipment.

## Refund timing
Refunds to e-wallets such as GoPay and OVO arrive within 1 to 3 business days. Refunds to credit cards arrive within 7 to 14 business days.

## Opened bags
Opened bags cannot be refunded unless the bag was damaged on arrival.
