# Shipping

## Shipping zones and fees
Shipping inside Jabodetabek is free and takes 1 to 2 business days. Shipping to Java outside Jabodetabek costs IDR 15,000 per shipment and takes 2 to 4 business days. Shipping to areas outside Java costs IDR 35,000 per shipment and takes 4 to 7 business days.

## Roasting and dispatch
Coffee is roasted every Monday and Thursday. Orders confirmed before 13:00 WIB on a roasting day ship the same day.

## Tracking
A tracking number is sent by WhatsApp and email when your box leaves the roastery.
